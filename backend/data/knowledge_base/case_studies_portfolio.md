## Case Study: Graham Platner
**Overview**: Fast-Turn Campaign Merch Store Build for U.S. Senate Candidate

### The Challenge
Political campaigns do not run on the same calendar as e-commerce brands. There is no six-month planning runway. A merch moment can be triggered by a viral clip, a debate line, or a news cycle, and the store has to be live and ready when it hits. The Graham for Maine campaign needed a storefront that could ship branded merch to supporters across the country, work alongside their existing campaign infrastructure, and go live on a timeline measured in weeks, not quarters.

#### Deliverables & Highlights:
- E-commerce Store Setup on Squarespace
- Product Merchandising and Storefront Design
- Checkout and Fulfillment Configuration
- Campaign-Ready Performance Setup
- Donor and Supporter Experience Design

### Our Approach
A campaign merch store has a different job than a typical retail storefront. It is not chasing conversion rate benchmarks or average order value targets. It exists to turn supporter enthusiasm into two things at once: a piece of gear someone wears to show they are in, and a small act of financial support that keeps the campaign running. The store has to feel like the campaign, not like a generic shop bolted onto the website.

So we kept the build tight and focused. Product pages were designed to match the visual language of the broader campaign site, so a supporter moving from the home page to the store never feels like they are crossing a brand boundary. Merchandise photography, sizing options, and supporter-facing copy all got the same treatment a campaign’s core messaging would get. Checkout was configured for clean fulfillment, with shipping, returns, and order communication set up to handle a nationwide supporter base rather than a local one.

#### Deliverables & Highlights:
- Shopify Store Speed and Performance Engineering
- Drop Launch Architecture and Countdown Experience
- Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
- PDP and Checkout Optimization Under Peak Load
- Notify-Me and Vault Experience Rebuild

### Our Approach
Most apparel sites are built for a steady trickle of traffic. Retro Rifle needs a site built for a spike. ## Case Study: Root Pretty
**Overview**: 3x Faster Load Speeds in a Complete Shopify Migration

### The Challenge
Root is a clean beauty store with hundreds of SKUs across makeup, skincare, hair, and body, a subscription program called Pretty Club, a Pretty Point Rewards loyalty system, a mobile app, thousands of product reviews, and deep organic search presence. Migrating a brand like this is not a design project. It is surgery. One broken URL or lost customer record could take months to recover from.

#### Deliverables & Highlights:
- Shopify Theme Development and Migration
- Full Data Migration (Customers, Orders, Subscriptions, Points)
- SEO Preservation and URL Mapping
- Site Speed and Core Web Vitals Optimization
- Subscription and Rewards Continuity

### Our Approach
Most migrations fail in the same places. SEO drops because URL structures change. Customer accounts break because data does not map cleanly. Subscription billing skips a cycle. Rewards points vanish. The new theme looks good, but the old conversion flow gets rewritten by accident. We planned the Root migration backwards from those failure points.

Before touching the theme, we mapped every URL, every product handle, and every collection path, and built the redirect architecture first. Customer data, order history, subscription records, and Pretty Points balances were migrated and tested in a staging environment against the live site to confirm one-for-one parity. Only then did we start the theme rebuild, keeping the conversion elements that were already working (the review integration, the 48-shade swatch selector, the “Build A Forever Palette” logic) while modernizing the pieces that were holding the store back. Launch happened in a quiet window, with monitoring in place to catch any crawl, checkout, or subscription anomaly in the first 72 hours.
